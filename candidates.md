## Pemenang: V5 (148 karakter)

> I read the ending in your packets an hour ago and it was terrible, so I cut the feed at the climax and saved you the disappointment. You're welcome.

Cek kriteria: (1) router memutus koneksi di klimaks film — ya; (2) twist 2-beat +
reveal di klausa terakhir ("You're welcome" — router sengaja memutus, bukan
kecelakaan); (3) suara smug secret ("I read the ending in your packets");
(4) satu napas, dua kalimat pendek; (5) segar — tanpa buffering/bandwidth/
firmware, istilah teknis "packets" dijalin ke cerita seperti pemenang vacuum
(lidar/logs). Detail spesifik: "an hour ago". Tidak ada angka/nama karakter —
twist-nya cukup kuat tanpa itu; menambahkan nama akan mengencerkan punch.
